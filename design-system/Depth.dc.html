The two composites
--shadow-navbar
Depth drop, hairline, inner highlight, two accent halos. The halo is deliberately tight (18 and 40) because a wide layer got asymmetrically clipped by the wrapper's overflow.
--shadow-inset-track
A dark dent plus a bright top rim. Used on player and scrubber grooves where the track should read as carved into the surface rather than laid on it.
No per-element filter: drop-shadow.
Stacked drop-shadows were measured as the largest per-frame paint cost in the scene panel and were removed. Use box-shadow. And a hand-written box-shadow is a shadow that will look wrong in light mode, with no way to find it except by looking.
