Mobile chrome: the dock
the one settled break from the floating-glass language
Below phone widths the desktop navbar is replaced, not shrunk: an edge-to-edge tab bar, 3.5rem content height, near-black, hairline top border, 12px blur, safe-area folded inside so it reads as device chrome. Burger tile opens the drawer; the dock (z 110) sits above the scrim (z 99) so the burger doubles as the close control. Width-driven, not touch-gated.
Icons: inline SVG, currentColor, never emoji.
Icons inherit their text colour and theme for free across eight accents and light mode. On Windows an emoji renders as a colour bitmap that ignores currentColor entirely, which is why the heart is drawn as bezier paths.
Voice: British English, sentence case, plain verbs.
Colour, customiser, organised. Labels name what the user controls, in their vocabulary: "Lite mode", never "disable backdrop-filter". A control says what it does. Errors say what happened and what to do. No emoji, no em dashes, anywhere.
