The floor is 5.0 and it is deliberate.
Below 5, a card is default glass. Do not "fix" this. If everything is a collectible, nothing is.
Escalation is monotonic.
Each tier adds to the one below rather than swapping to a different idea, so a user can rank two cards by looking at them.
The top three are accent-independent.
Diamond reads as cold crystal, Legendary as mythic, Perfect as full spectrum, regardless of which accent the user has set. The chromatic shift away from their accent is what signals rarity.
Rank sets z-order.
Bronze 20 through Perfect 70, so a higher tier's halo is never clipped by a lower-ranked neighbour. No isolation and no will-change: those promote each card to its own layer, which Chrome sometimes paints out of z-order.
